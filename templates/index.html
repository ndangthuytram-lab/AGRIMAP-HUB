CoreLogic AI

Business Strategy & Expansion Engine

Executive Summary

CoreLogic AI is an advanced system designed to act as an automated Chief Marketing Officer (CMO) and Expansion Strategist. It empowers SMEs and family-owned businesses to scale beyond their immediate geographical boundaries using data-driven infrastructure.

Core Modules

  • AI Demand & Inventory Predictor: Calculates inventory adjustments based on real-time weather and events to prevent overstocking and understocking.
  • Global Market Benchmark: Compares your wholesale price against a real global commodity benchmark (USDA NASS egg prices, converted at the live USD/VND exchange rate), with strategic context on where you sit relative to it.
  • Strategic Distributor Map: Queries real business locations from OpenStreetMap to find and score potential B2B wholesale buyers within a designated radius, empowering sales prioritization.
  • Smart Route & Logistics Optimizer: Resolves Vehicle Routing Problems (VRP) using real geocoding and live road-network routing, sequencing deliveries to minimize distance while guaranteeing arrival within strict vendor time windows.
